# Post-deploy smoke-test prompts

Date: 2026-06-22

Small, intentionally **vague** prompts to paste into the product after every
deploy — the kind a real first-timer with no technical background would type.
Lowercase, no spec, no constraints, occasional typo. The point is to see what the
agent does when it has to fill in *all* the blanks: pick a layout, invent the
copy, choose colors, screenshot-verify, maybe deploy — with almost nothing to go
on.

Grab a few at random each deploy. If any of these produces something broken,
blank, or "coming soon", that's the bug.

## More detailed (a user who's thought about it a little)

These have a bit more to chew on — a vibe, a few sections, some real content —
without crossing into a full spec. Good for testing layout choices, longer copy
generation, and multi-section pages.

- make a landing page for a coffee shop called Bean There in portland. cozy and
  warm, show the menu (latte, cold brew, drip), opening hours, and where we are
- build a website for my bakery Bakin' Cakes. i want a hero with a photo, a
  section for our cupcakes and breads, customer reviews, and a way to order for
  pickup
- portfolio site for a freelance illustrator named Mara. clean and minimal,
  a grid of my work, an about me, and a contact form
- landing page for a SaaS called Tasklytics that helps teams track work. needs a
  headline, 3 features, pricing (free / pro / team), and a sign up button
- one page site for a restaurant called Saffron in nyc. indian food, show a menu
  with a few dishes and prices, hours, reservations, instagram link
- make a site for a dog walking business Paws & Go. friendly and playful, list
  the services and prices, show the areas we cover, and let people book
- build a personal blog for someone who writes about hiking. a list of posts,
  a sidebar about the author, and make 3 fake posts to fill it in
- landing page for a fitness app called Iron Hour. dark theme, energetic, show
  the app on a phone, list workout features, and pricing with a free trial
- product page for a pair of headphones called Echo One. big product shot,
  specs, a few highlight features, price, add to cart
- event page for a music festival called Sunfall in the desert. lineup of fake
  bands, dates, ticket tiers, an FAQ, and a countdown to the event
